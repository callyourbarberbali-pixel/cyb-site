Story

The chair comes to you.

CYB started with a simple idea: the best cut shouldn't depend on a queue or a commute. So the shop goes mobile — a full kit, a fresh towel, and a barber who shows up at your door, on schedule, every time.

Whether it's a villa above the Ubud rice fields, a guesthouse in Penestanan, or a retreat in Tegallalang, you get the same unhurried, premium experience you'd expect from a proper chair — minus everything that gets in the way of it.

One barber. Full attention. Your space, your music, your time.

Good To Know

Mobile barber, answered.

How much does a haircut in Bali cost with CYB?
A men's haircut is Rp 300K, beard trim Rp 100K, and a full straight-razor shave Rp 150K. Hair colour and perm start from Rp 600K. Travel to your villa, hotel or home is included within our service areas, so the price you see is the price you pay.
Which areas of Bali do you cover?
CYB is a mobile barber covering Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu, Nusa Dua and Ubud, plus the surrounding areas. Further out? Send us a message and we'll usually make it work.
Do you really come to my villa or hotel?
Yes. Sunny arrives with the full kit, fresh blades and everything needed to set up at your villa, hotel room, or home across Bali. No traffic, no waiting room, just your chair and your time.
How do I book a mobile barber in Bali?
Booking is done in one message on WhatsApp. Tap any "Book" button on this page, tell us your service, area and preferred time, and we'll confirm. Same-day appointments are often available.
What services do you offer?
Haircuts and fades, beard trims, straight-razor shaves, hair colour and perms. Real barbering, done at your place by appointment.
How far in advance should I book?
Whenever suits you. We take same-day bookings when there's a slot, but for a specific day or time, a message the day before is the safest way to lock it in. Hours are Mon–Fri 10am–8pm and Sat 10am–6pm.

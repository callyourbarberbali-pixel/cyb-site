WhatsApp Setup Guide

Quick & easy — for Sunny 💈

Why do this? When a customer messages us on WhatsApp, we want to reply fast and look professional. This setup gives you ready-made answers (prices, areas, booking) that you can send with one tap. Faster replies = more bookings. It takes about 10 minutes to set up once.

Step 1 — Get the WhatsApp Business app

This is a different app from your normal WhatsApp. It is free.

  1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Play Store (Android).
  2. Search “WhatsApp Business” and install it (green icon with a “B”).
  3. Open it and sign in with your number +62 896-7011-9008.
  4. It will ask to move your chats over — tap Yes / Continue.
✅ After this, use WhatsApp Business for the barber business. Your chats stay the same.

Step 2 — Set the Welcome (Greeting) message

This message sends automatically when a new customer messages you first.

  1. Open WhatsApp Business.
  2. iPhone: tap Settings (bottom right).   Android: tap ⋮ (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Business tools.
  4. Tap Greeting message → turn it ON.
  5. Delete the old text and type the message below. Set recipients to “Everyone”. Tap Save.
WELCOME MESSAGE
Yo! 👋 Welcome to CYB — Bali's mobile barber. We bring the full barbershop to your villa, hotel or home 💈 Drop your area + when you'd like your cut and we'll get you sorted!

Step 3 — Set the Away message

This sends automatically when you are busy or asleep, so customers know you'll reply soon.

  1. In Business tools, tap Away message → turn it ON.
  2. Type the message below.
  3. For schedule, choose “Always send” (or set your own hours). Tap Save.
AWAY MESSAGE
Thanks for messaging CYB! 💈 Sunny's probably mid-cut right now — we'll get back to you very soon. To speed things up, send your area + preferred day & time.

Step 4 — Add the Quick Replies

These are saved answers. In a chat you type “/” then the shortcut word, and the full message appears.

  1. In Business tools, tap Quick replies.
  2. Tap + (or “Add”).
  3. Paste the Message and type the Shortcut shown for each one below.
  4. Tap Save. Repeat for all 7.

Step 5 — How to use a Quick Reply in a chat

  1. Open a chat with a customer.
  2. Tap the message box and type / — your saved replies appear.
  3. Tap the one you want (e.g. /prices) → the full message fills in → press send. ✅
⭐ The most important habit: After every single cut, send the /review message while the customer is happy. More Google reviews = we show higher on Google = more customers find us. This is the #1 thing that grows the business. 🙏
